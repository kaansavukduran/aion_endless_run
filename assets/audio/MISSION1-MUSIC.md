# Mission 1 soundtrack trial

Both original Ogg tracks are by **MintoDog** and are published under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

- `mission1-forest.ogg` plays during most of Mission 1. The canal and roots keep their dedicated ambient loops. [Forest Stage](https://opengameart.org/content/forest-stage)
- `mission1-boss-battle.ogg` begins when the Root Engine joins combat. [8bit Action Boss Battle](https://opengameart.org/content/8bit-action-boss-battle)

The supplied Vorbis files are unchanged. Godot loops the files in play and follows the existing audio level, mute and pause settings.
