# Game checks

## Protect play while editing UI

- Open the real game through the affected entry point. Exercise the relevant path through load, menu, gameplay, pause/resume, result, retry/back and recovery. Check state persistence and existing saves or input mappings with a disposable profile. A successful build or title screen is only evidence for that step.
- Verify menus and HUD labels remain visible in the supported viewport and orientation. Inspect actual use of the playfield, safe area, readability, visible focus and clickable controls. Match spacing, contours, icon optical alignment and hit areas for equivalent actions. Keep intentional variants for differing priorities. Confirm active input hints match real bindings and overlays do not swallow gameplay or leave held actions stuck after release/cancel.
- Exercise supported keyboard/mouse, touch, and gamepad flows. For a touch change, verify independent fingers, press/release/cancel, rotated layout and gesture conflicts with page scroll where relevant. For gamepad, verify directional focus, confirm/back, rebind and reconnect where applicable. Software-injected inputs do not verify a physical controller. When input handling changed, hold an input across pause, blur, disconnect or touch cancellation as relevant, then resume and verify release, focus, and the first new action.
- For changes that affect graphics or motion, apply the [sprite animation checks](sprite-animation.md). Preserve approved asset sources, palette, geometry, controls, and game rules. Any input, collision, save, timing, or asset regression needs behavioral evidence. For retry, scene reload, or repeated pause/resume, verify scores, timers, and event listeners update once and old overlays or input handlers no longer act. Exercise the actual trigger when a test can skip gameplay through a debug helper.

## Ground contact and scenery seams

- For in-scope scenery, verify that trees, cacti, rocks, buildings and other grounded props meet the visible surface that supports them. Inspect the visible trunk base, root or footing at play scale. Check for exposed gaps, sinking, abrupt terrain seams, and visible/collision disagreement where the surface is interactive. A transparent sprite margin, decorative shadow, or the image rectangle's lower edge gives weak evidence of contact.
- Identify the support surface in the prop's own depth layer. For a slope, ledge or stepped terrain, resolve support at the placed anchor. A background or parallax layer can have a different surface from the player's road or floor. Check a prop and its support in the same coordinate space, including parent transform, scale, crop/pivot, camera projection and rounding. Keep deliberately airborne pickups, flying creatures and authored floating scenery in their intended states.
- Fix the shared placement, trim/pivot metadata, layer transform, or support geometry that causes the gap. If the separation is baked into background artwork, fix the art or compositing layer with the project's approved sources. Preserve intentional depth and keep gameplay collision unchanged for decorative props. When the surface or prop is interactive, test its visual contact and physical support separately.
- Recheck representative instances at normal play scale and while the camera moves. Include affected slopes, section joins, parallax repeats, biome changes, viewport scales and generated placements where present. For swaying foliage, step through the frame loop and verify the visible base holds while upper foliage moves. For recurring defects, assert the transformed contact against the intended visible support using the project's tolerance and inspect a rendered view at the reported conditions. Report source inspection, measured geometry, and observed rendering at their actual scope.

## Godot and web exports

- Read the project's scene, theme, container tree and target stretch settings. Inspect Control rects, combined minimum sizes, container size flags, clipping, theme/style margins, wraps and focus neighbors after layout settles. Compare ScrollContainer range and child bounds where applicable. Look for a parent size cap or incorrect layout before replacing nodes. A long text screen may use existing pagination if it preserves content and works with supported inputs. Test changes to focus and clipping in the scene and actual game; keep deliberate clipping such as a masked playfield.
- If the project targets web, check the exported entry rather than relying on editor success. When affected, verify cold and warm launch, real load progress or an honest indeterminate state, failure/retry, first playable input, audio activation, focus recovery and build identity. For an update/cache change, test old-to-new migration and saves with disposable data. For an export-only failure, isolate missing files, resource errors, runtime compatibility or stale build with evidence. Report browser/GPU limitations and genuinely tested browsers separately. Do not deploy as part of local verification.
- Keep native desktop, browser, browser emulation and physical phone/controller evidence separate. Use the project's documented browser and device targets. For a packaging or release QA request, verify the actual target artifact and version with its authorized route.
